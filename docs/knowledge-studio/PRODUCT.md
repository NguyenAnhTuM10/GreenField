# Knowledge Studio — Product direction

## Long-term goal
Turn structured knowledge into engaging, visually varied 9:16 educational videos for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Target formats include:
- Science: "1 mol là gì?", "Vì sao nước đá nổi?", physics/astronomy explanations.
- English: Vietnamese-learner mistakes, IELTS Speaking collocations, sentence building, recall.
- Short explanatory/news/quiz content.

The product is NOT a fixed slide template with words swapped. Different lesson goals should lead to different narrative beats, compositions and visual techniques. Prioritize actual understanding: misconception, prediction, explanation, visual proof/application, recall.

## Video design principles
- Text/real user-provided images/animated hand-drawn SVG first; AI-generated image/video later.
- Scientific diagrams must be accurate or explicitly identified as conceptual illustrations.
- Leave viewers a genuine pause to think or answer; pacing must not blindly equal audio duration.
- Hook must not misrepresent science or facts.
- Users approve storyboards before expensive automated production in later stages.
